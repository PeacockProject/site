Own your device

Not a toolkit. Your phone, given back.

From building it, to slipping past the lock, to the day a system crashes and quietly recovers itself — every part exists for one reason: the device you paid for should answer to you, not its manufacturer.

The path

From a locked slab to truly yours.

Four steps, one continuous thread — not four separate products. This is how a phone becomes yours, end to end.

01 · Build

Make the OS your phone should've shipped with.

Pick your device, choose how you want it — point-and-click or every flag on the command line — and out comes a complete system cut to fit that exact phone.

02 · Unlock

Slip past the factory lock.

Every phone guards what's allowed to boot. Two keys get you in — one for MediaTek, one for Qualcomm — exactly where the stock loader refuses.

03 · Live

A real computer in your pocket — that stays yours.

Run actual Linux. Swap the whole system for a different flavor tomorrow and your apps, files and settings don't move — they sit on a foundation the OS just rides on top of.

04 · Recover

If it ever breaks, it catches itself.

A recovery layer boots on its own the moment something won't start — rescue, repair, reinstall, right from the phone in your hand. You're never left with a brick.

And it's not only phones. The same system boots on a laptop or desktop too. Open source, no accounts, nothing phoning home — yours, all the way down.
